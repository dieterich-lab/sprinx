# Notes related to data curation

## Evidence tier values for metadata TSV file

Tier values record the strongest class of experimental evidence supporting arm
absence for a given fixture entry. Assign the highest tier that applies.

### Tier 1: direct biochemical confirmation at the RNA molecule level

Mature tRNA transcript directly sequenced at RNA level (including RACE-confirmed
end mapping); or structure confirmed by enzymatic or chemical probing on purified
native tRNA; or aminoacylation demonstrated with purified native tRNA and cognate
synthetase.

### Tier 2: transcript physically confirmed; molecular identity partially established

Mature transcript confirmed to exist by Northern blot or size fractionation,
and/or tRNA physically isolated by gel or hybridisation selection but not
sequenced at RNA level; or modification identity confirmed by 2D-TLC or mass
spectrometry on purified native tRNA.

### Tier 3: genomic sequence inference only

Arm absence inferred computationally from inability to fold the gene sequence
into the relevant stem, with downstream sequence encoding an adjacent gene; no
RNA-level evidence beyond transcription being plausible.
